### Description (4000 char limit)

Pumping is hard work. Your tracking app shouldn't be.

Pump Coach was designed by the IBCLC lactation team at The Breastfeeding Mama specifically for pumping moms — whether you're exclusively pumping, building a stash for your return to work, or navigating combo feeding.

TRACK SESSIONS YOUR WAY
• Live session timer with pause and resume — leave the app and pick up where you left off
• Log manually when it's 3 AM and you just want to be done
• Track left and right sides separately, or total output only
• Record your pump settings for massage and expression phases, so you can see what actually works
• Letdown quality, pain levels, and notes for every session

KNOW YOUR NUMBERS
• Today's output at a glance, with your daily goal front and center
• 7-day trends, session quality scores, and output-per-hour compared to typical
• Find your best time of day and most productive pump settings
• Insights written for real life — encouraging, not anxiety-inducing

MANAGE YOUR MILK STASH
• Log every bag with location: fridge, freezer, deep freezer, or room temp
• Automatic expiration tracking based on storage guidelines
• Alerts before milk expires — never lose those precious ounces
• Stash goals for returning to work, trips, or weaning, with the math done for you

SHARE WITH YOUR SUPPORT TEAM
• Invite your partner or lactation consultant to view your data (read-only)
• IBCLCs get a full client dashboard: 90-day history, trends, and shared notes
• You stay in control — revoke access anytime

EXTRAS THAT MATTER
• Flange Fit Check: guided sizing help, because the wrong flange changes everything
• Pump reminders that work around your schedule
• Hydration tracking
• oz and ml, always

PUMP COACH PREMIUM
Upgrade for the AI-powered coach, advanced analytics, stash goals, and personalized insights. Free trial included, cancel anytime.

From the team behind The Breastfeeding Mama, trusted by hundreds of thousands of moms. We built the app we wished our clients had.

Note: Pump Coach provides educational information and tracking tools. It is not a substitute for medical advice. Always consult your healthcare provider or a lactation consultant for concerns about feeding or supply.

## Privacy questionnaire quick answers (both stores)

Data collected: email (account), health-related data (pumping/nursing logs — user-provided), identifiers (user ID).
- Data IS linked to identity (account-based).
- Data is NOT sold, NOT shared with third parties for advertising.
- Data is encrypted in transit; users can request account + data deletion in-app (Profile → Delete account).

App Store "App Privacy" section:
- Health & Fitness → Linked to you → App functionality
- Contact Info (email) → Linked to you → App functionality
- Identifiers (User ID) → Linked to you → App functionality

Play "Data safety" section:
- Personal info: email — collected, not shared
- Health info: user-entered logs — collected, not shared
- Security: data encrypted in transit, deletion available
